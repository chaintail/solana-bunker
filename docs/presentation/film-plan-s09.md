## 4. Look recipes

- **1990s infomercial, problem half:** 4:3 inside scene 8's CRT, the bezel as pillarbox. Grayscale with lifted contrast, a slight blur (VHS softness), animated grain, faint 6 px scanlines, a soft vignette, glass inner shadow and rounded corners (45/39 px, scene 8's corners at 1.5x). Captions: "DRAMATIZATION" (Inter 800, 30 px, letter-spaced, white on a 62% black strip, lower left) and "THERE HAS TO BE A BETTER WAY!" (Oswald 700, 54 px, white with a black outline, across the top). Push-ins are fast eased snap zooms, the cheesy kind, on the zoom layer only.
- **Colour snap:** one white frame, then everything at once: grayscale off, grain off, scanlines off, glass off, the bars fall away, and the set hard-cuts to the studio. The post grain stops at the same frame (ffmpeg `noise` with `enable='lt(t,18.60)'`).
- **Product shot:** a saturated blue starburst (28 rays from (690, 330), slow rotation of 2° per second), an edge darkening, the key-card grid as the backdrop. The BIP-39 card is glossy (yellow-orange gradient, a dark outline, a white shine band sweeping across once after it lands, Anton 108 px blue with a white outline and a dark extrude). Parchment: cream-to-tan radial gradient, a fractal-noise fibre overlay, a seeded ragged edge, "RECOVERY SEED" in Liberation Serif, printed numbers, words in Architects Daughter ink.
- **Mnemonic:** `data/s09-mnemonic.js` from `pipeline/s09/mnemonic.py`. It is a valid 24-word BIP-39 English mnemonic from fixed public entropy (SHA-256 of a fixed string plus the first counter giving 24 distinct words); the checksum is verified by round trip. The wordlist is vendored at `pipeline/s09/bip39-english.txt` and checked against the canonical SHA-256 (2f5eed53…). The phrase is unfunded and carries no "example" or "demo" caption: odor assume hurdle safe unknown refuse barrel laptop theory bind diamond security struggle feature say happy suspect worth search ramp tumble digital rocket speed.
- **Post (ffmpeg on dev-mac, with the audio mux):** light luma grain `noise=c0s=7:c0f=t+u` before the snap only, `vignette=angle=PI/4.6` throughout (as scene 8). Master x264 crf 14; phone 960x540 crf 26.
