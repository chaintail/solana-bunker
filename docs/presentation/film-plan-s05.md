   s05-v6-x12-phone.mp4 (960x540 crf 26).
2. Pause after "if it matches": c7b is split at the -45 dB dip between "matches" and "the" (source 235.625 s) into c7b ("If it matches") and c7b2 ("the vault
   lets you in"), 20 ms fades on both cuts, PAUSE_7B = 0.36 scene s between them (= 300 ms heard at 1.2x; voice track silent, the bed covers it). The head holds
   the last "matches" frame through the pause. Every phrase from c7b2 on, and every cue keyed to them, moves 0.36 s later; total 96.42 -> 96.78 s
   (data/s05-edl.json, s05-timing.js, s05-audio.filter regenerated by s05-timing.py). Head sheets regenerated with s05-heads.py: pixel-identical to v5
   (the split falls on a source frame boundary).
3. Lab coat pose switches (standing rule): pipeline/s05-pose-align.py measures every lab coat sprite and writes assets/s05/pose-align.js; setPose draws
   each pose PNG shifted by its dx/dy so the collar centre, shoulder line and baseline coincide, and all lab poses share one head anchor (shrug's oval
   centre 511.5 / chin 308). Reference pose = shrug (the last pose, so the hand-off frame to slide 6 is unchanged). Keys whose framing was designed with the
   unshifted professor-point PNG (S4_END_POSE under the slide-4 frame, the two edge pokes) are pin()ned: their pivot moves back by the screen image of dx.
