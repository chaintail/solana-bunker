## Beat 2, the hash vault (whiteboard, deliberately cheap)
An invisible marker writes (stroke-reveal, no hand) the title "HASHES", then a stick man top left (the user), a big rectangle on the right titled "Bunker Program 001". The one high-quality object: a black machined block labelled MATH with "sha256 · hashv syscall" in small print. See-the-code box top right (GitHub QR to program/src/lib.rs line 113 on main) and the proof box under it (Solscan QR to devnet withdrawal `5mHxtbGL…`, full signature in artifacts/public-deployment.json), as on every program-version reveal. Secrets list down the left edge: secret_1, secret_2 … secret_1000 (abbreviate with … after ~8, last line secret_1000). The thousand-square grid draws row by row inside the program box. Cascade: secrets peel off the list, fly through MATH, come out as hashes that snap into squares; starts one at a time, accelerates to a blur, stops dead on the last; list text dims. Spend: one secret lights, moves to the stick man, then to the program, red X over its square, a Solana coin bounces back to the stick man. "Pretty simple, eh?": lab coat shrug pose. Rent: a price tag swings down off the grid, "33,126 bytes", rolls over to "0.234 SOL"; "RENT" stamped. Lab coat teleports (cut, not slide) and flips to the right side when pointing at the program box; he may vanish for stretches and poke in diagonally from an edge to point at a new element.

## Beat 4, the robbery

Liam's head tinted red with devil horns and a bandit mask pops up from the bottom edge (a fourth version of him; no body needed).
